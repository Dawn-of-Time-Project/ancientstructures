**Ancient Structures** is a Minecraft mod that generates hand-built structures from ancient cultures in the overworld. Explore Germanic farms, Roman temples, Japanese pagodas and Mayan pyramids, face the guardians who protect them and loot the armor and decorations hidden inside.

Part of the **[Dawn of Time](https://www.curseforge.com/members/dawnoftime_team/projects)** mod ecosystem.

<div align="center"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/yhha6Zo.png"></div>

## Features

- **22 structures** across 4 cultures: Germanic, Roman, Japanese and Pre-Columbian (Mayan).
- **Biome based generation**: each culture spawns in the biomes that match its style.
- **Ancient guardians**: 9 armored mobs wearing Armor of the Ages sets, some of them bosses.
- **Cultural loot**: food, decorations from Dawn of Time and rare armor pieces, different for every culture.
- **Advancement tree**: discover every structure and defeat every guardian.
- Several biome mods compatibility (Terralith, Biomes O' Plenty, Regions Unexplored, Nature's Spirit, Atmospheric)

| **Japanese** | Taiga, snowy taiga, old growth taigas, cherry grove | Pagoda, temple, Rengoku house |

## Guardians

Guardians are skeletons, wither skeletons and zombies equipped with the armor of their culture. Each one gives an advancement.

| Culture | Guardian |
|---|---|
| **Germanic** | **Cursed Knight** (Iron Plate armor), **False Archmage** (Holy armor, wither skeleton) |
| **Roman** | **Centurion** (Centurion chestplate), **Praetorian** (full Centurion armor, wither skeleton) |
| **Japanese** | **Fallen Samurai** (O-Yoroi armor), **Corrupted Farmer** (Bamboo Hat), **Temple Guardian** (O-Yoroi and Raijin helmet, wither skeleton) |
| **Pre-Columbian** | **Tribesmen** (Quetzalcoatl helmet), **Wrath of Quetzalcoatl** (Quetzalcoatl armor, wither skeleton) |

## Loot

Every culture has its own chests, with a common table and a rare table.

| Culture | Common loot | Rare loot |
|---|---|---|
| **Germanic** | Food, candles, books, leather | Holy and Iron Plate armor, bows, gold |
| **Roman** | Dawn of Time clay tiles, marble flower pots and fences | Centurion armor, bows, gold |
| **Japanese** | Teapots, sake, tatami mats, roof tiles, raked gravel | O-Yoroi, Raijin and Japanese Light armor, bows, gold |
| **Pre-Columbian** | Cocoa, moss, vines, melon slices, rammed dirt | Quetzalcoatl armor, bows, gold, cobwebs |
| **Ruins** | Old iron, leather and diamond armor pieces, bones, skulls | Name tags, diamonds |

## Ancient Structures Series

Ancient Structures is a **series of mods** that adds thematic and cultural structures to Minecraft. Each mod focuses on one culture, and together they bring more lore and depth to the world. They share the same logic: structures, guardians and loot tied to a culture.

| Mod | Theme | Authors                       | Links |
|---|---|-------------------------------|---|
| **Ancient Structures** | Germanic, Roman, Japanese and Mayan cultures | TheGoldenWorld, Jackie        | [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/ancient-cultural-structures), [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/ancient-structures) |
| **Ancient Structures: Edo Japan** | Japan during the Edo period (1603-1868) | Structures by **Knoxxturre**  | [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/ancient-structures-edo-japan), [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/ancient-structures-edo-japan) |
| **Ancient Structures: Chinese** | Chinese history | Structures by **Jackiefr**    | [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/ancient-structures-chinese), [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/ancient-structures-chinese) |
